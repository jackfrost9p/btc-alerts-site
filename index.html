BTC Streak Alerts

BTC Streak Alerts is an automated notification service operated by John Finn, a sole proprietorship. A software application monitors public cryptocurrency exchange data and sends an SMS alert when BTC-USD closes three consecutive 15-minute candles in the same direction — three rising or three falling.

Alerts are sent only to subscribers who have opted in by texting the keyword below. No phone numbers are collected from any other source.

How to subscribe

Text the keyword START to NA — the same number the alerts come from. You will receive a confirmation reply immediately:

John Finn BTC Alerts: You are now subscribed to BTC price alerts. Msg frequency varies, up to [N] msgs/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

Texting the keyword is the only way to subscribe. No one is enrolled by default, and no phone numbers are obtained from any other source, purchased list, or third party.

What a message looks like

John Finn BTC Alerts: BTC-USD up 3 GREEN 15m candles close $118,400 | streak +0.62% | last +0.21% Reply STOP to end.
John Finn BTC Alerts: BTC-USD down 3 RED 15m candles close $116,950 | streak -0.74% | last -0.19% Reply STOP to end.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with market activity. You may receive up to [N] messages per day. Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping messages

  • Reply STOP to any message to end all messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email jackfinnstocks@gmail.com.

Not financial advice

These alerts report that a price pattern occurred. They are not a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and the pattern has not been shown to predict future prices.
