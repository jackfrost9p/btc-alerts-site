The service

BTC Streak Alerts is an automated SMS notification service operated by John Finn, a sole proprietorship. A software application sends a text message when the price of Bitcoin (BTC-USD) closes three consecutive 15-minute candles in the same direction.

Consent

Subscribers opt in by texting the keyword START to NA, the same number the alerts are sent from, and receive an immediate confirmation reply. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by replying STOP.

Message frequency and cost

Frequency varies with market conditions. You may receive approximately 30 messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Carrier charges are the recipient's responsibility.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP to any message to end all messages. Other accepted opt-out keywords are STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, and QUIT. You will receive one confirmation and nothing after it.
  • Reply HELP or INFO for assistance, or email jackfinnstocks@gmail.com.

Not financial advice

These messages report that a price pattern occurred. They are not investment, financial, or trading advice, and they are not a recommendation to buy or sell any asset. Three candles closing in the same direction is a common occurrence and has not been shown to predict future prices. Any trading decision is the recipient's own.

Accuracy and availability

Price data comes from a third-party public exchange feed and may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. Alerts may be missed entirely during network outages, exchange downtime, or when the sending system is offline. The service is provided as-is, with no warranty of accuracy, timeliness, or availability, and no liability is accepted for any loss arising from its use or from a message that arrives late or not at all.
