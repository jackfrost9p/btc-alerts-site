What this is

BTC Streak Alerts is a personal software project. A program watches public exchange data and sends its author a notification when Bitcoin (BTC-USD) closes three consecutive 15-minute candles in the same direction.

No service is offered

Nothing here is offered to the public. There is no sign-up, no account, no subscription, and no support obligation. These pages describe a private tool; they are not an invitation to use one, and no alerts are sent to anyone other than the author.

Not financial advice

These alerts report that a price pattern occurred. They are not investment, financial, or trading advice, and they are not a recommendation to buy or sell any asset. Three candles closing in the same direction is a common occurrence and has not been shown to predict future prices. Any trading decision is the reader's own.

Accuracy and availability

Price data comes from a third-party public exchange feed and may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. Alerts may be missed entirely during network outages, exchange downtime, or when the sending system is offline.
