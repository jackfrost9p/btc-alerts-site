Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8/20/26

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers or SMS consent to any third party for any purpose.

Mobile information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information is shared with any third party, including subcontractors or affiliates, except as strictly necessary to transmit the messages themselves.

Who operates this service

BTC Streak Alerts is operated by John Finn, an individual sole proprietor located in Dallas, Tx, United States. Questions about this policy can be sent to jackfinnstocks@gmail.com.

What information we collect

A mobile phone number, and nothing else. We receive it only when a person texts the keyword START to our number to subscribe. It is used solely to deliver the alerts described on this site.

We do not collect names, email addresses, payment details, location data, browsing activity, or any information from visitors to this website. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

How consent is collected

Subscribers opt in by texting the keyword START to 1-708-905-2260, the same number the alerts are sent from. This is an affirmative action taken by the subscriber. No one is enrolled by default, and no numbers are purchased, rented, or obtained from any third party or list.

How the number is used

The number receives automated SMS notifications when Bitcoin closes three consecutive 15-minute candles in the same direction. It is used for no other purpose. It is not added to any marketing list, because no marketing list exists.

Who the number is shared with

The number is transmitted to Twilio Inc. only as the messaging carrier required to deliver the SMS, and to the mobile carrier that terminates the message. Those parties process it solely to deliver the message and may not use it for their own marketing. No other person or company receives it.

Message frequency

Frequency varies with market conditions. You may receive approximately 30 messages per day. Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop messages

  • Reply STOP to any message to end all messages. You will receive a single confirmation — "John Finn BTC Alerts: You have been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe." — after which no further messages are sent.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or email jackfinnstocks@gmail.com.

Data retention

Message delivery records are retained by Twilio under its own retention policy and are used only to confirm delivery and diagnose failures. Numbers are deleted on opt-out.

Children

This service is not directed to anyone under 18 and no information is knowingly collected from children.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
